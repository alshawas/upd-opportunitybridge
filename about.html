Why this exists

Access starts with knowing an opportunity exists.

OpportunityBridge is an independent student project built around a simple problem: students with fewer advising resources often spend more time just discovering the programs that better-connected students hear about automatically.

The bridge

From “I did not know that existed” to “I have a real shot at this.”

The design goal is to shorten the distance between a student's background and the programs built to support students like them.

Discovery → fit → deadline → official source
The idea

Opportunity discovery is an access problem too.

I built OpportunityBridge after seeing how scholarship, fellowship, internship, and college-access information is scattered across separate websites, mailing lists, counselors, social media, and professional networks. For a student balancing school, work, family responsibilities, or limited access to advising, simply finding the right program can become a barrier of its own.

The goal is not to replace official program websites or make eligibility decisions. It is to make discovery faster: surface programs a student may not know exist, explain why one could fit, show the timing clearly, and send the student directly to the official source with enough time to act.

Design principles

What the experience is optimized for.

Less hunting

Bring high-value programs into one searchable place instead of sending students to dozens of disconnected lists.

Clear fit signals

Explain why a listing rose in the ranking without pretending a score is an admissions decision.

Privacy by default

Make sensitive profile details useful for matching without requiring them to become an account or marketing profile.

Time-aware

Surface current, upcoming, and rolling programs with transparent timing instead of stale evergreen lists.

Data standards

How opportunities are verified.

The database is curated around programs that can be checked against a current official source. Current-cycle information comes from the organization running the program, not scraped reposts.

Official sources first

Every listing links to the organizer, application portal, government page, or official admissions page used to verify it.

Current-cycle checks

The current data snapshot was researched for the 2026–2027 cycle and verified on September 4, 2026.

Transparent uncertainty

If a program has not posted an exact deadline, the listing says that instead of inferring or inventing one.

Deadlines and criteria can change after verification. OpportunityBridge is a discovery tool, so the official source always controls.
Privacy by default

Your background does not need to become a database.

The matching experience runs entirely in the browser. This version has no account system, analytics profile, or backend storing the answers you select.

Profile answers stay local

Education level, financial context, and optional eligibility details are used only by JavaScript on your device to rank the opportunity cards.

Saved programs stay local too

Bookmarks use browser local storage. Clearing site data removes them, and there is no server-side student profile behind the interface.
