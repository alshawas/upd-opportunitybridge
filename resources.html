A simple four-week sprint

Work backwards from the deadline.

You do not need a complicated productivity system. You need enough margin to get feedback and avoid submitting everything at 11:58 p.m.

4 weeks outRead eligibility, gather documents, choose recommenders.
3 weeks outDraft essays and identify the strongest examples.
2 weeks outGet feedback, tighten resume, verify requirements.
1 week outFinal review, upload early, confirm submission.
